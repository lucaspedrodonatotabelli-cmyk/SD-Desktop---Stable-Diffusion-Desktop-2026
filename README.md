### Issue 2: The App Won't Startor Crashes on Open

This is almost alwaysa GPU driver issue. 

**Solution:**
- Right-click your desktop,and select**"NVIDIA Control Panel"** (if you have NVIDIA**. 
- Go to"Help" >"Updates" andclick"Check for updates". 
- Downloadand installany available driver updates. 
- Restart your computer andtry launching SD-Desktopagain. 

If you have an AMD card,search"AMD driver update"in Windows update orvisit the AMD website. Keeping your GPU driver current is the single best thing you can do for performance.



### Issue 3: Model Download Stuck at 0% or Slow

Model files are huge (multi-GB**,and sometimes your internet connection may struggle. 

**Solution:**
- Pause the download,andresume it after 30 seconds. 
- Check that your router is not blocking large downloads. 
- Try connecting your computer via Ethernet cable instead of Wi-Fi for faster speeds. 
- Alternatively,close the app entirely,and reopen it. Downloads will resume automatically.
